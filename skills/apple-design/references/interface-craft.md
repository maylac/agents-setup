# Interface craft

Use translucent materials as hierarchy, not decoration: content can scroll beneath floating chrome; heavier material separates structure, lighter material marks interaction. Do not stack light glass surfaces. Animate blur and scale together when material appears. Keep text over changing material high-contrast and slightly heavier.

For reduced motion, replace slides, springs, and parallax with short opacity changes or static state. For reduced transparency, use an opaque surface and remove blur. For increased contrast, use near-solid backgrounds and clear borders. Large moving surfaces should become less visually dominant while travelling.

Type scales optically: tighten large display text, keep body tracking near zero, use tighter leading for headings and looser leading for body text. Use scalable spacing and a platform system font unless a custom face has a clear role.

Give feedback at its causal event. Sound and haptics belong on meaningful commits, success, errors, and snaps, synchronized with the visual response. Keep focus visible and immediate; use clear labels and place controls close to what they change.
